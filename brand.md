# Brand — Privia

_Status: active_

Confidential invoice financing on the Canton Network. The product should read
as a real fintech tool: clean, confident, quiet. Privacy is the product —
the UI's job is to make per-party visibility legible, not decorated.

## Palette

| Token        | Value     | Use                                        |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| brand        | `#14685A` | Primary actions, active states, focus ring |
| brand-dark   | `#0C1E1B` | Headlines, dark panels (privacy strip)     |
| background   | `#FAF7F2` | Warm off-white app background              |
| brand-soft   | `#E3EFEC` | Tinted surfaces, amount badges             |
| alert        | `#B3261E` | Rejection moments only — never decoration  |

Persona accents (identity, used sparingly — avatar, active pill, view border):
Supplier `#14685A` · Buyer `#8C5E2A` · Financier A `#2F5A8C` · Financier B `#6B4A8C`.

## Typography

Hanken Grotesk (UI, via next/font) · Geist Mono for numbers/party IDs
(`tabular-nums` on anything that changes).

## Voice

Plain English, active voice, no crypto jargon. Privacy claims must be driven
by the real ACS — never fabricate what a party "cannot see".
